## 3. Recent Cybersecurity Incident: The CrowdStrike Falcon Outage
- **What happened:** In July 2024, a routine sensor configuration update pushed to CrowdStrike’s Falcon platform triggered logic errors on millions of Windows endpoints, resulting in widespread Blue Screens of Death (BSOD) and crippling critical services like airlines, hospitals, and payment systems worldwide.
- **Why it caught my attention:** People usually think of cybersecurity failures strictly in terms of external attackers or zero-day exploits. This incident showed that defensive software running at kernel level with direct rings of privilege can become a massive single point of failure if testing pipelines and staged rollouts break down. It made me realize how critical site reliability and secure update architecture are to overall operational resilience.
